    Frequently asked questions

    Is the image resizer free to use?

    Yes, resizing images on PDFpress is completely free. There are no limits on the number of images and no sign-up required.

    Are my images uploaded to a server?

    No. All images are processed locally in your browser using your device’s own processing power. Nothing is ever uploaded, which keeps your images 100% private.

    What image formats are supported?

    You can resize JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP and WebP images, and save the result as JPG, PNG or WebP.

    Will resizing reduce image quality?

    Reducing the pixel dimensions slightly softens fine detail, which is normal. For photos, JPG at 90% quality gives an excellent balance of size and quality.

    Can I resize several images at the same time?

    Yes! Select or drop up to 20 images and they will all be resized with the same settings. Download them one by one or all together.
