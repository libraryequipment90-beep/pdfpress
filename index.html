    Frequently asked questions

    Is PDFpress free to use?

    Yes, compressing PDFs on PDFpress is completely free. There are no limits on the number of files and no sign-up required.

    Are my PDF files private and secure?

    Absolutely. Your files are processed on our servers only to create the compressed version, and they are automatically deleted 30 minutes after processing. We never share or sell your data.

    Will compression reduce the quality of my PDF?

    We use smart compression that reduces file size while preserving the visual quality of your document. For documents with heavy images, try the “Recommended” or “Less” compression level to keep maximum quality.

    What is the maximum file size I can upload?

    Each file can be up to 150 MB, and you can upload up to 20 files at once. There are no daily limits.

    Can I compress several PDF files at the same time?

    Yes! Select or drop multiple PDFs and we will compress them all in a single batch. Download them one by one or as one ZIP file.
