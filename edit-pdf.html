Frequently asked questions

Is the PDF editor free to use?

Yes, editing PDFs on PDFpress is completely free. There is no sign-up required and no limit on how many files you edit.

Are my PDFs uploaded to a server?

No. Your PDF is read and edited locally in your browser using your own device. Nothing is ever uploaded, which keeps your documents 100% private.

Can I edit text that is already in the PDF?

PDFs store text in a fixed layout, so rewriting existing text isn’t possible. Instead you can add, move and remove new text on any page, which is how most online PDF editors work.

Will the layout of my PDF change?

No. Only the text you add is included in the download. Images, fonts and page layout stay exactly as they were.

Can I edit more than one page?

Yes. Every page of your PDF is shown as a thumbnail in the sidebar — click one to open it and add text.
